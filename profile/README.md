Canessa System Labs builds software, AI agents and skill systems with a stricter standard: every product keeps a record of what it did, what it checked and what still needs a human decision.

**Build in private. Present with intent.** Implementation stays private. What appears here is curated: the problem, the idea, how it works and the evidence, released through a reviewed gate.
